EvalGen · Shankar et al., UIST 2024 · re-run with Claude 5.5

Grade the outputs. Watch EvalGen choose its checks.

These are the two LLM pipelines from the EvalGen paper. Grade each output Good or Bad. After every grade, EvalGen picks the candidate check for each criterion that best matches your grades, and scores the set. Every check has already been run on every output, so selection happens instantly in your browser.

The reference grades were written by Claude under a fixed rule set. They stand in for the two authors who graded every output in the paper, so treat agreement with them as agreement with a stand-in, not with a person. Your grades help replace them.

Outputs: Claude Haiku 5.5. Candidate checks: written by Claude Opus 5.5 and run on every output; LLM checks run with Haiku 5.5. Evaluator agents: Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5, shown either the prompt only or the prompt plus the criteria. Paper: doi.org/10.1145/3654777.3676450
